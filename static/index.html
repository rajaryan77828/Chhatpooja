01 / THE SPIRIT OF CHHATH

A prayer to the
setting & rising Sun.

Chhath Puja is a sacred festival dedicated to Surya Dev and Chhathi Maiya. It is observed with deep devotion, discipline and gratitude, as families come together to offer Arghya to the setting and rising Sun.

More than a festival, Chhath is a beautiful expression of nature worship, inner strength, family bonds and the spirit of community.
